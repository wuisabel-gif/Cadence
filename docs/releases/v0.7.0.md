# Cadence v0.7.0

Released October 26, 2026. **Scores change** from v0.6.0.

## Highlights

- **A new signal: vocabulary variety.** AI prose repeats words less than people
  do. Text of 40 words or more gains up to 21 points when its moving-average
  distinct-word ratio passes 0.93.
- **Score the clipboard from any app.** In the desktop app, copy text and press
  Cmd+Shift+. (Ctrl+Shift+. on Windows and Linux).
- **Modern human text in the benchmark.** 80 passages written before ChatGPT's
  launch now guard against rules that only flag modern writing.

## Accuracy

The rule was tuned on development data, then scored once on a held-out set
that was frozen before the work began:

| detector | AI caught | human left clean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v0.6.0 | 2 of 35 | 30 of 35 |
| v0.7.0 | 5 of 35 | 30 of 35 |

Recall is still low; most AI text without the stock tells grades A. That
held-out set also showed more human false alarms than earlier sets. Uniform
rhythm and triads cause most of them, and one triad match counts "even" as a
list item. Those fixes wait for a new frozen set, so this result stays honest.
The exact reports are in [benchmark/reports](../../benchmark/reports/).

## Upgrade notes

Scores from 0.6.0 and 0.7.0 are not comparable, and results now carry
`detectorVersion: "0.7.0"`. Results also gain `metrics.vocabVariety` and
`breakdown.structural.variety`. If you store scores or gate on a cutoff, pin
the version:

```bash
npx --package=cadence-deslop@0.7.0 cadence-install-skill --agent kimi
npx --package=cadence-deslop@0.7.0 cadence-install-codex
```

Replace `kimi` with `zcode`, `claude-code`, `opencode`, or `grok` as needed.

## Desktop installers

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) | `.dmg` |
| Windows | `.msi` or `.exe` |
| Linux | `.deb`, `.rpm`, or `.AppImage` |

The builds are not code-signed yet. On a Mac, open System Settings → Privacy &
Security and choose **Open Anyway**. On Windows, choose **More info → Run
anyway**. `Cadence_universal.app.tar.gz` is a zipped copy of the Mac app that
most people can ignore.
